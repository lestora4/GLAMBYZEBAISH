  • Dedicated support before and after your appointment

Q.

How do I register as an artist?

+

Joining Glam by Zebaish as a makeup artist is simple:

  • Visit our Artist Registration page
  • Fill in your details, portfolio, and ID proof
  • Submit your application for review
  • Once approved, log in to your artist dashboard to start accepting bookings

Need help? Reach out to us at +91 9505777790 for enquiry and booking support.

Q.

Do you take bookings outside Hyderabad?

+

Currently, we are accepting bookings in Hyderabad, Bangalore and Kochi.

For any enquiry or booking through the website, contact us at +91 9505777790.

+

No, we do not provide a makeup trial.

Q.

What is included in the Signature Glam Makeup package?

+

The Signature Glam Makeup package (₹2,999) includes:

  • Full-face party makeup
  • Skin preparation and base makeup
  • Eye makeup with lashes (if required)
  • Lip makeup
  • Basic hairstyling
  • Makeup setting for longer wear

Standard products are used in this package.

Q.

What is included in the Sangeet Glam&Glow package?

+

The Sangeet Glam&Glow package (₹4,999) includes:

  • Full-face makeup using professional products
  • Skin preparation and base application
  • Eye makeup with lashes
  • Lip makeup
  • Basic hairstyle/styling
  • Makeup setting for enhanced longevity

Perfect for brides, bridesmaids, sisters, and family members who want an elegant and festive look for the Sangeet ceremony.

Q.

What is included in the Royal Groom Styling package?

+

The Royal Groom Styling package (from ₹2,499) includes:

  • Groom makeup and skin finishing
  • Hair styling
  • Beard grooming and setting
  • Premium, long-lasting look

Perfect for wedding grooms, engagement ceremonies, reception events, traditional functions, and photoshoots.

Q.

What is included in the Reception Glam package?

+

The Reception Glam package (from ₹8,999) is a luxurious, bespoke beauty experience that includes:

  • Soft contour and luminous highlight
  • Long-wear, camera-ready finish

  • Dupatta / veil draping and setting

What is included in the Bridal Signature Package?

+

The Bridal Signature Package (from ₹8,999) is a complete bridal transformation that includes:
